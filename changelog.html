更新日志Changelog

0.1.3

2026 年 11 月 20 日November 20, 2026

Ibo 现在可以自己更新

  • 有新版本时会弹出更新窗口，可以立即安装、跳过这个版本或稍后提醒，安装后重新启动即可
  • 托盘菜单新增“检查更新…”
  • 设置的“关于”页可以关闭自动检查更新，也可以立即检查
  • 0.1.2 及更早的版本不能自动更新到 0.1.3，请从官网下载安装一次

Ibo now updates itself

  • When there is a new version, a window offers to install it now, skip it or remind you later, and the update runs after a relaunch
  • New in the tray menu: “Check for Updates…”
  • Turn the automatic check off, or check now, in Settings under About
  • Version 0.1.2 and earlier cannot update to 0.1.3 by themselves: download and install it once from this site

0.1.2

2026 年 11 月 20 日November 20, 2026

学习模式

  • 结果旁的 ♡ 或 ⌘D 把句子收进词汇本，可以顺手记下场景，收错了能撤销
  • ⇧⌘L（Windows 为 Alt+Shift+L）把弹窗切换成复习卡片：看中文写英文、听写、改错、连词成句四种题型，逐词作答，答完显示句子结构和评级
  • 新的学习窗口：今日、词汇本（搜索、筛选、分组、编辑、导出 JSON 或 CSV）和统计（过去一年的练习、近 30 天评分分布、最近练习）
  • 句子结构按成分着色，标出音标和释义，点击单词朗读，右键把单词或词组收进词汇本
  • 设置新增“学习”页：每天复习和新学的数量、每日开始时间、题型、朗读与场景；托盘显示今天还剩几条待复习，可以关闭

其他改进

  • macOS 版通过 Apple 公证，下载后可以直接打开
  • 弹窗始终显示在其他窗口之上，不会再被前台应用挡住
  • macOS 上 Ibo 常驻 Dock，点 Dock 图标打开翻译弹窗
  • 从 0.1.1 升级后，需要在“系统设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 辅助功能”中重新允许 Ibo

Learn Mode

  • Click ♡ beside a result or press ⌘D to keep a sentence in the notebook, with a note about where you read it, and undo if you change your mind
  • ⇧⌘L (Alt+Shift+L on Windows) turns the popup into a review card with four exercises: write it from the Chinese, dictation, correction and sentence building. Answer word by word, then see the sentence’s structure and your grade
  • A new Learn window: Today, the notebook (search, filter, group, edit, export as JSON or CSV) and statistics (a year of practice, grades over the last 30 days, recent practice)
  • Sentence structure is coloured by role, with phonetics and meanings. Click a word to hear it, or right-click to keep a word or phrase
  • A new Learn page in Settings: reviews and new entries per day, when the day begins, exercises, read-aloud and notes. The tray shows how many reviews are left today, and this can be turned off

Other improvements

  • The macOS version is notarized by Apple and opens straight after download
  • The popup always stays above other windows instead of being covered by the app in front
  • On macOS Ibo stays in the Dock; click its icon to open the translation popup
  • After upgrading from 0.1.1, allow Ibo again in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility

0.1.1

2026 年 11 月 7 日November 7, 2026

Ibo 有了新名字和新图标

  • AI 翻译与英文检查：改过的词有下划线，附中文意思和修改说明，可以补充原意重新检查
  • 检查选中文本后，⌘↩ 把改好的文字替换回原来的应用；原文已经变化时不会覆盖
  • 原文、译文和逐句朗读，美式与英式英语各四种音色
  • 快捷键改为点击录制，可以停用或一键恢复默认；Windows 默认改为 Alt+1、Alt+2、Alt+3 和 Alt+Shift+G
  • 系统开启“减少透明度”时，弹窗改用不透明背景
  • 出错时可以展开“错误详情”，便于反馈问题

Ibo has a new name and a new icon

  • AI translation and English checking: changed words are underlined, with the meaning in Chinese and the reasons, and you can add what you meant to check again
  • After checking a selection, ⌘↩ replaces it with the corrected text in the app it came from, unless the original has changed
  • Read aloud the original, the translation or one sentence at a time, in four American and four British voices
  • Record shortcuts by pressing them, turn any off, or restore the defaults at once. The Windows defaults are now Alt+1, Alt+2, Alt+3 and Alt+Shift+G
  • With Reduce Transparency on, the popup uses a solid background
  • When something fails, Error Details show what to include in a report
